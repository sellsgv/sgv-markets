How to read a report

Three numbers that tell you who has the leverage

Months of supply

How long it would take to sell every listed home at the current pace. It is the quickest read on whether buyers or sellers are in control.

Under 4 favors sellers · 4 to 6 is balanced · Over 6 favors buyers

Sale vs. original list

What sellers actually received compared to their first asking price. It shows how much negotiating and how many price cuts it took to sell.

100% means sold at the first asking price

Price per square foot

The fairest way to compare homes of different sizes. When the median price rises but price per foot does not, bigger homes are selling, not higher values.
