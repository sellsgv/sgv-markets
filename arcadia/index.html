The Arcadia Market

What Arcadia homes are actually selling for, how fast they move, and who has the leverage right now. Straight from the MLS, with our read on what it means for you.

The market right now

Arcadia is a balanced market, leaning toward buyers.

At September's pace, it would take about 5.2 months to sell every single-family home currently listed. That is near last summer's high of 5.5 months, and more than the region as a whole (3.6 months across all of CRMLS).

Prices

Prices are holding at about twice the regional median.

The typical Arcadia single-family home sold for about $2.0 million this summer (July through September), up from about $1.82 million a year earlier. Across all of CRMLS, the single-family median has barely moved, holding near $925,000.

Arcadia only has 15 to 35 sales a month, so one month's median can swing a lot. September's $2.215 million is real, but we would not call it a new normal yet. The chart smooths each line into a 3-month average for that reason.

Value per square foot

Bigger homes are selling. The price per foot is not rising.

Here is the detail most reports miss. Over the last 12 months, Arcadia's median price went up 4.6% while its price per square foot went down 4.7%, from $785 to $748.

That means the mix of homes selling shifted toward larger houses. The same house is not worth 4.6% more than last year. If you are pricing your home off a neighbor's headline sale, the price per foot is the fairer comparison.

Supply and demand

More homes are coming to market. Fewer are going into escrow.

From July through September, 145 Arcadia homes were newly listed, up from 133 the same months last year. Only 74 went pending, down from 97.

When new listings run ahead of new escrows, inventory builds and buyers get more choice. September's pending count has already been revised from 11 to 13 as late entries came in, and may tick up a little more, but the gap has been widening all summer.

Leverage

Months of supply: who holds the cards.

Under 4 months of supply usually favors sellers. Between 4 and 6 is balanced. Above 6, buyers have the edge.

Arcadia spent this past winter in seller territory (2.8 months in February and March) and climbed into balanced territory through the summer. It reached 5.2 months in September, close to the 5.5-month peak of June 2025. The region as a whole has stayed between 2.2 and 4.3 months the entire time.

What sellers actually get

Your home is worth what the market pays, not what an app says.

Over the last 12 months, Arcadia sellers averaged 97.7% of their original asking price. Only 3 of those 12 months reached 100%. Across all of CRMLS, single-family sellers averaged 99.2%, so Arcadia buyers are negotiating harder than most.

This past winter was the low point: 93.3% in January 2026. On a $1.8 million home, that is about $120,000 below the first asking price.

The blue line shows what sellers got compared to their final list price. In September, Arcadia homes sold at 98.0% of their last price but only 96.5% of their original one. That gap is price reductions. Homes priced right on day one tend to land close to asking. Overpriced homes end up chasing the market down.

Timing

When the Arcadia market actually moves.

Across 2023 to 2025, homes that sold in April and May got the most for their sellers: 101.7% and 100.3% of original list on average, with a median of just 13 to 14 days on market.

For sellers, that points to listing in late February or March, so you are in escrow during that spring window. For buyers, January has been the most negotiable month, averaging 95.8% of original list. Homes that sat through the winter and closed in March also sold well under their first price, at 96.1%.

Straight answers

The Arcadia market, in plain terms

Is Arcadia a buyer's market or a seller's market right now?

Balanced, leaning toward buyers. Arcadia had 5.2 months of single-family supply in September 2026. Under 4 months favors sellers, over 6 favors buyers.

How many homes are for sale in Arcadia?

126 single-family homes were active at the end of September 2026, up from 118 a year earlier and 75 as recently as March.

What is the median home price in Arcadia?

Monthly medians over the last year averaged about $1.88 million. The summer of 2026 (July to September) averaged about $2.0 million, and September alone was $2.215 million.

Do Arcadia homes sell for asking price?

Close to it. Over the last 12 months, single-family sellers averaged 97.7% of their original list price and 98.5% of their final list price. Well-priced homes still sell near asking, and overpriced ones usually take a reduction.

How long does it take to sell a home in Arcadia?

The median home that sold in September 2026 was on the market 25 days, up from 14 in August. Spring sales move fastest, typically 13 to 14 days.

When is the best time to sell in Arcadia?

Spring. Sales that closed in April and May brought the highest prices relative to asking from 2023 to 2025. Listing in late February or March lines you up for that window.
